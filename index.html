THIS IS REEFBOUND

A SURVIVAL WORLD THAT KEEPS CHANGING

REEFBOUND is built around one idea: the players should help create the world they survive in. Explore, build, trade, fight, cooperate and leave your mark.

01

EXPLORE

Discover procedural islands, hidden beaches, jungle paths, shipwrecks, caves, ruins and dangerous waters.

02

SURVIVE

Manage resources, shelter, equipment and danger while weather, darkness and enemies change the rules.

03

BUILD

Turn a tiny raft into a floating home, fortified outpost or entire player-made settlement.

04

SHAPE

Trade, form alliances, create rivalries and help define what happens in the shared world.

THE WORLD

NOT JUST AN ISLAND.
A WHOLE OCEAN.

Leave the safety of your starting point and cross an unpredictable world of islands and open water. Every journey can reveal something new.

  • Procedurally generated islands and exploration zones
  • Open ocean travel and floating settlements
  • Jungles, beaches, cliffs, caves and ancient ruins
  • Underwater exploration, reefs and wrecks
  • Dynamic day/night atmosphere and dangerous weather
  • Hidden resources, rare locations and secrets
